One afternoon a family went to the roller rink. In the corner, under the disco lights, stood a Crossy Road arcade cabinet.

Dad watched the kids play a round and thought: I bet I could build my own game like that with Claude, in a single session.

So that evening he did. The chicken hopped, the cars came, and the first version was playable before dinner was cold.

Then the kids got to play it. And the kids had ideas.

A tilt to peek in 3D. Coins that pay for the peeking. Hidden tunnels through the hedges. Eggs that hatch into followers who trail you like a snake. Boats, submarines, alligators. Planes you can ride on the wing. Trains with crossing gates. A hearing at the Department of Pedestrian Grievances at the end of every day. Insert coin to continue.

Most of it went in the same day.
